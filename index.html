How I Work

A process built for high-stakes decisions

01

Discover the real problem

I start with stakeholder alignment and user research before touching a wireframe. Problems that look like UX problems are often business or data problems in disguise.

02

Frame with clarity

Define the right problem space with information architecture and design principles before visual execution. Ambiguity at this stage costs 10x later in development.

03

Build iteratively

Rapid prototyping in Figma with continuous engineering alignment. I embed AI tools in my workflow for faster concept velocity, not as a replacement for judgment.

04

Measure what matters

Usability testing, analytics review, and stakeholder feedback close the loop. Design decisions are hypotheses. Measuring makes them real.

The Person Behind the Work

Engineer turned designer. Thinker who builds.

I started with a Mechanical Engineering degree from Dr. J.J. Magdum College. Then I discovered that the most interesting problems in engineering are the human ones. That realisation led me to MIT Institute of Design in Pune, where I spent three years learning how to think about products at a systems level.

In 2013, I joined Think Design Collaborative in Bengaluru, working across Fortune 500 clients in finance, healthcare, and logistics. In 2015, I moved to Globant in Pune, where I have led UX engagements across six industry verticals over a decade.

I am not a designer who dabbles in strategy. I am a strategic thinker who leads with design. I have run discovery workshops with C-suite stakeholders in the morning and shipped Figma prototypes to engineering by end of day. I mentor junior designers, shape hiring decisions, and build design cultures that outlast individual projects.

Outside work: I am an aviation enthusiast. There is something about the discipline of cockpit UI design, where a bad decision costs lives, that I find profoundly instructive for enterprise UX. Clarity under pressure. No room for ambiguity. Every interaction accountable.
